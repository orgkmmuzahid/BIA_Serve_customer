# BIA Serve Customer App

## Overview
The **BIA Serve Customer App** is designed to simplify logistics, vendor sourcing, and delivery tracking for users in Tanzania. The app offers a seamless experience for placing orders, tracking deliveries, verifying vendors, and making payments.

This bilingual (English/Swahili) mobile application caters to urban shoppers, busy professionals, and upcountry senders, enabling them to manage deliveries and vendor services with ease.

## Key Features
- **Place Orders**: Select delivery services, pickup zones, and delivery zones. Upload product images and select delivery time slots.
- **Track Orders**: Real-time tracking for deliveries, with notifications on pickup, transit, and delivery statuses.
- **Vendor Verification**: Verify vendors by uploading conversation screenshots and paying a verification fee.
- **Purchase & Delivery**: Buy items from vendors and have them delivered directly to the user.
- **Bulk Orders**: Upload bulk order lists, negotiate prices, and manage dispatching with the help of admin.
- **Ratings & Feedback**: Rate the service after each delivery, and flag any issues for admin review.
- **Payments**: Supports mobile payments via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, and more.
- **Support**: Contact support directly through the app for assistance with orders or issues.

## Languages Supported
- **English**
- **Swahili**
